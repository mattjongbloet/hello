About

Build the right thing, and build it right.

I’m Matt, a rigorously curious Senior Staff Product Designer at Hospitable who enjoys simplifying the complex through design. I live in Amsterdam.

My story

It began with street art: colour, composition and form. It’s all about communicating a message that’s clear, concise and beautiful. How do you get there?

I always knew I wanted to use design to make people’s lives a little better. The first years of my career, in visual and brand design, left me feeling like I was making nice-looking things without a purpose.

So I left Canterbury, England 🧙 for an MSc in Digital Design in Amsterdam 🇳🇱. There I learned the fundamentals of lab, field and showroom research, and experimented with new methods on real projects for large companies. My next step was to find a place where I could deliver dev-ready designs and also conduct research.

I got lucky. After marrying my now wife, I moved to the USA 🇺🇸, where I played pivotal design roles for 5+ years at early- and mid-stage SaaS companies. I’ve since moved back to Amsterdam, where I help hosts run their short-term rentals on autopilot at Hospitable.

My work centres on understanding the problem deeply, through empathy, user research and strategy, before designing the solution. I feel an obligation to myself, my colleagues and my clients to put my experience, energy and empathy into something valuable, well executed, and that resonates with the people it’s for.
