Increasing trust and confidence in home value estimates

Every financial insight Homebot gives starts from the estimated value of your home. I redesigned how that number is explained, so homeowners could trust it.

Context

The number every insight depends on

  • Homeowners receive a monthly home report with actionable insights based on their monthly home valuation.
  • The majority of these financial insights use the estimated home value as their baseline.
  • The home value experience had been designed two years earlier and had several usability and usefulness issues.

Opportunity

Trust in the estimate is trust in the product

Insights from a variety of sources (UserVoice, customers, clients, Homebotters, data, usability markup and competitor analysis) pointed to an opportunity to increase trust in our home values. That would empower homeowners and customers to make more informed financial decisions, and so increase trust and confidence in our product.

Approach

Sketch, test with 60 people, plan three sprints

  • Explored broadly. I sketched and rapidly prototyped variations on copy, animations, infographics, visualisations and interactions.
  • Tested with 60 people. I ran generative and evaluative usability sessions with 60 participants, landing on a design where 9 out of 10 participants had a general understanding of the calculation and more confidence in its accuracy.
  • Planned delivery with engineering. With the engineering leads I broke the design into three sprints, each the smallest chunk of deliverable value.

Outcome

A solution the whole company had confidence in

  • I presented the project to the entire company using the Double Diamond framework, giving them confidence in a solution to a poorly performing experience and showing how the product org works.
  • The iteration was in the product backlog and had not reached production when I left Homebot.
