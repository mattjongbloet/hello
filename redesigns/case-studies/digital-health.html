Context

A marketplace that needed funding

Human API wanted to acquire funding to build a digital health marketplace, and needed a compelling story to convince investors.

  • Consumers who visit healthcare providers and create accounts with Human API demonstrate a willingness to use and leverage the system.

Opportunity

Show people what their health data can do for them

Human API believed that, given access to their health data and guidance on how to use it, consumers would change their behaviour and make purchasing decisions based on it.

But consumers were unaware of the benefits they could get from personalised savings based on their health data, such as medical ID theft protection, coordinated drug cost savings and special payment programmes for healthcare debt.

Approach

One day, one story, one prototype

  • A one-day workshop. I ran a workshop focused on defining the key persona’s journey and the problem to solve, ideating and sketching possible solutions, and confirming the direction through prototyping.
  • Co-created with the CEO and COO. Together we created the core user journey and persona, and identified the opportunity areas.
  • Wireframes, then a prototype. I created low-fidelity wireframes and a high-fidelity interactive prototype of the chosen solution for presentation to investors.

Outcome

A story the CEO and COO could take to investors

The CEO and COO felt confident in the story, the prototype and the presentation slides they would use to acquire funding for a digital marketplace.
