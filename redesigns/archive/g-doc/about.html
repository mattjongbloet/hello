But the first five years of my career, in visual and brand design, left me feeling like I was creating nice-looking things without a purpose.

My story

  1. Canterbury, England 🧙. Five years of visual and brand design.
  2. Amsterdam, NL 🇳🇱. An MSc in Digital Design, where I learned the fundamentals of lab, field and showroom research and experimented with new methods on real projects for large companies.
  3. United States 🇺🇸. I got lucky. After marrying my now wife I moved to the USA, where I played pivotal roles in design for 5+ years at early- and mid-stage SaaS companies, delivering dev-ready designs and running research.
  4. Amsterdam, NL 🇳🇱. Back again, helping hosts run their short-term rentals on autopilot at Hospitable.
