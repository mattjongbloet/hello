Helping homeowners drop mortgage insurance early

Around a million Homebot homeowners were eligible to remove private mortgage insurance early. I designed the feature that told them, and connected them with their loan officers and realtors.

Overview

Interviews and our data warehouse pointed to the same opportunity: help homeowners realise savings on private mortgage insurance (PMI), and help our customers, their loan officers and realtors, build trust as advisors.

We estimated that around one million US homeowners using Homebot were eligible to remove PMI early, saving around $195 a month each. Together, roughly $200 million a month.

2

Background

  1. Home prices rose faster than ever in 2021. The typical home gained $50,000 in value.
  2. Nearly two-thirds of US households struck by COVID-19 faced financial trouble.
  3. PMI lets buyers without a 20% down payment become homeowners. It can be removed once the loan-to-value ratio falls below 80% of the original appraised value, or sooner if the home has appreciated.
3

Action

  1. I sketched ideas and rapidly prototyped variations on copy, infographics and visualisations, and on ways to connect with loan officers and realtors.
  2. I ran usability sessions with 20 participants, landing on a design rated 4.8 out of 5 on its value and on confidence in removing PMI.
  3. With the engineering team leads, I broke the design into four sprints, each the smallest chunk of deliverable value.
4

Outcome

  1. Within the first sprint, homeowners sent more than 500 requests to learn about cancelling PMI, saving them around $97,500 a month.
  2. I presented the project to the whole company using the Double Diamond framework. It connected colleagues to our mission, showed how the product org works, and built excitement for the feature.
