Homebot · Consumer Team · July 2022

One screen. $200M a month.

It starts with one homeowner and one question: when can you remove your mortgage insurance? For an eligible homeowner, the answer was worth around $195 a month.

101
Validate

20participants

I sketched and prototyped variations on copy, infographics and visualisations, and on ways to reach loan officers and realtors. Then I tested with 20 participants: the final design scored 4.8/5 on value and confidence in removal.

102
Ship

500+requests

With the engineering team leads I broke the design into four sprints, each the smallest chunk of deliverable value. In the first, homeowners sent 500+ requests to learn about cancelling PMI, worth around $97,500 a month in savings.

106
The opportunity

≈1Mhomeowners

Interviews and our data warehouse showed about a million Homebot homeowners were eligible to remove PMI early. 2021 made it timely: home prices rose faster than ever (the typical home gained $50,000), while nearly two-thirds of COVID-hit US households faced financial trouble.

$108
The whole

≈$200Ma month

≈1M homeowners × ≈$195 a month: roughly $200 million a month in potential savings, and a reason for every loan officer and realtor to reach out. I presented it to the whole company using the Double Diamond.
