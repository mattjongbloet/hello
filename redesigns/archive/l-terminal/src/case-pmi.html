NAME

pmi — help ≈1 million US homeowners drop private mortgage insurance early

SYNOPSIS

pmi [--explore] [--validate n=20] [--plan sprints=4] [--align scope=company]

DESCRIPTION

Interviews and our data warehouse pointed to the same opportunity: help homeowners realise savings on private mortgage insurance (PMI), and help Homebot’s customers, their loan officers and realtors, build trust as advisors.

We estimated around 1,000,000 eligible homeowners saving around $195/month each: roughly $200,000,000/month in potential savings.

ENVIRONMENT

Home prices rose faster than ever in 2021; the typical home gained $50,000 in value.

Nearly two-thirds of US households struck by COVID-19 faced financial trouble.

PMI lets buyers without a 20% down payment become homeowners. It can be removed once loan-to-value falls below 80% of the original appraised value, or sooner if the home has appreciated.

OPTIONS

--explore
Sketched ideas and rapidly prototyped variations on copy, infographics and visualisations, and on ways to connect with loan officers and realtors.
--validate
Usability sessions with 20 participants. Final design rated 4.8/5 on value and on confidence in removing PMI.
--plan
With the engineering team leads, broke the design into 4 sprints, each the smallest chunk of deliverable value.
--align
Presented to the whole company using the Double Diamond framework: connected colleagues to the mission, showed how the product org works, built excitement for the feature.

EXIT STATUS

0 — In the first sprint, homeowners sent 500+ requests to learn about cancelling PMI, worth around $97,500/month in savings.
