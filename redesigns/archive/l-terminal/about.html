# About

I always knew I wanted to use design to make people’s lives a little bit better. The first five years of my career, in visual and brand design, left me feeling like I was making nice-looking things without a purpose.

So I left Canterbury, England for an MSc in Digital Design in Amsterdam. I learned lab, field and showroom research, and tried new methods on real projects for large companies. Then I looked for a place where I could deliver dev-ready designs and also run research.

After marrying my now wife I moved to the US, where I played pivotal design roles for 5+ years at early- and mid-stage SaaS companies. I’m now back in Amsterdam as Senior Staff Product Designer at Hospitable, helping hosts run their short-term rentals on autopilot.
