Senior Staff Product Designer · Hospitable

Matt Jongbloet

Matt has more than ten years of experience in product design. He holds an MSc in Digital Design (Cum Laude) and a UX certification from Nielsen Norman Group.

He started in visual and brand design in Canterbury, England, and left after five years feeling he was making nice-looking things without a purpose. An MSc in Amsterdam gave him the research to change that. He then spent more than five years in pivotal design roles at early- and mid-stage SaaS companies in the US, including Homebot and Trellis.

He has since returned to Amsterdam, where he leads design at Hospitable, helping hosts run their short-term rentals on autopilot.
