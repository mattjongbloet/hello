Case file F1HomebotJuly 2022Consumer Team

Helping homeowners realise they can drop mortgage insurance early

Methods: interviews · data warehouse analysis · rapid prototyping · usability testing (n = 20)

Abstract

Interviews and our data warehouse showed that around one million Homebot homeowners were eligible to remove private mortgage insurance (PMI) early, saving roughly $195 a month each. I prototyped and tested ways to tell them, and to connect them with their loan officers and realtors. The final design scored 4.8/5 with 20 participants, and the first sprint generated more than 500 requests to learn about PMI removal.

I presented this project to the whole company using this same framework

Discover

What interviews and the data warehouse told us

Two sources pointed at the same opportunity: helping homeowners realise savings on PMI, and helping Homebot’s customers, their loan officers and realtors, build trust as advisors. The context made it timely:

  • Home prices rose faster than ever in 2021. The typical home gained $50,000 in value.
  • Nearly two-thirds of US households struck by COVID-19 faced financial trouble.
  • PMI lets buyers without a 20% down payment become homeowners. It can be removed once the loan-to-value ratio falls below 80% of the original appraised value, or sooner if the home has appreciated.
Define

Sizing the opportunity

We estimated how many Homebot homeowners could act on it, and what it was worth to them.

Develop

Prototype widely, then test with 20 people

  • I sketched ideas and rapidly prototyped variations on copy, infographics and visualisations, plus different ways to connect homeowners with their loan officers and realtors.
  • I ran usability sessions with 20 participants, landing on a design rated 4.8/5 on its value and on confidence in removing PMI.
Deliver

Four sprints, each one valuable on its own

With the engineering team leads, I broke the design into four sprints, each the smallest chunk of deliverable value.

500+requests to learn about PMI cancellation in sprint one
≈$97.5ka month in savings from those requests
1company-wide presentation, using the Double Diamond

The presentation connected colleagues to our mission, showed how the product org works, and built excitement for the feature.
