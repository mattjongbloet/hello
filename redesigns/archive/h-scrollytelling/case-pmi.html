Homebot · Consumer Team · July 2022

When can you remove your PMI?

That’s the question this feature answers for homeowners. About a million Homebot users were eligible to remove private mortgage insurance early. I designed how we told them, and how we connected them with their loan officers and realtors.

My part: prototyping, usability research, delivery planning.

01 · Context

Why it mattered

+$50ktypical home’s value gain in 2021
≈⅔of COVID-hit US households in financial trouble
80%loan-to-value: below it, PMI can come off

Home prices rose faster than ever in 2021, and the typical home gained $50,000 in value. Nearly two-thirds of US households struck by COVID-19 faced financial trouble.

PMI lets buyers without a 20% down payment become homeowners. It can be removed once the loan-to-value ratio falls below 80% of the original appraised value, or sooner if the home has appreciated.

02 · Opportunity

A million homeowners, $195 a month each

≈1Meligible Homebot homeowners
× $195saved per homeowner, per month
≈$200Ma month in potential savings

Interviews and our data warehouse pointed to the same opportunity: help homeowners realise PMI savings, and help Homebot’s customers, their loan officers and realtors, build trust as advisors.

We estimated that around one million homeowners on Homebot could remove PMI early, saving around $195 a month each. Roughly $200 million a month in total.

03 · Prototype

Explore copy, visuals and ways to connect

I sketched ideas and rapidly prototyped variations on copy, infographics and visualisations, and on ways to connect homeowners with their loan officers and realtors.

04 · Test

20 participants, 4.8 out of 5

4.8 / 5

Value and confidence in removing PMI, across 20 participants.

I ran usability sessions with 20 participants, landing on a design rated 4.8/5 on its value and on confidence in removing PMI.

05 · Deliver

Four sprints, each valuable on its own

With the engineering team leads, I broke the design into four sprints, each the smallest chunk of deliverable value.

06 · Outcome

500+ requests in the first sprint

≈$97.5ka month in savings from those requests

Within the first sprint, homeowners sent more than 500 requests to learn about cancelling PMI. I presented the project to the whole company using the Double Diamond framework, connecting colleagues to our mission and building excitement for the feature.
