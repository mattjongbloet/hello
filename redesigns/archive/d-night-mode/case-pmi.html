When can you remove your PMI?

The question I designed Homebot’s mortgage insurance feature around, for the ≈1 million US homeowners we estimated were eligible to remove it early.

01 — Context

Why 2022 was the moment

Interviews and our data warehouse pointed to the same opportunity: help homeowners realise savings on private mortgage insurance, and help Homebot’s customers, their loan officers and realtors, build trust as advisors.

+$50kThe typical US home’s gain in value in 2021, when prices rose faster than ever.
≈⅔of US households struck by COVID-19 faced financial trouble.
80%loan-to-value: below it, PMI can be removed, or sooner if the home appreciated.
02 — Opportunity

Sizing it

PMI lets buyers without a 20% down payment become homeowners. With prices rising, many of ours could already remove it.

≈1Meligible homeowners×$195per month each≈$200Ma month in potential savings
03 — Process

Explore, test, slice

  1. Prototype widely. I sketched and rapidly prototyped variations on copy, infographics and visualisations, and on ways to connect homeowners with their loan officers and realtors.
  2. Test with real people. Usability sessions with 20 participants landed on a design rated 4.8/5 on its value and on confidence in removing PMI.
  3. Plan delivery with engineering. With the engineering leads I broke the design into four sprints, each the smallest chunk of deliverable value.

04 — Outcome

500 requests in one sprint

Within the first sprint, homeowners sent more than 500 requests to learn about cancelling PMI, worth around $97,500 a month in savings.

I presented the project to the whole company using the Double Diamond framework, connecting colleagues to our mission, showing how the product org works, and building excitement for the feature.
