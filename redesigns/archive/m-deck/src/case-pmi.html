Next four slides take each diamond half in turn.

01 · Discover

2021 changed the maths for homeowners.

+$50ktypical US home’s gain in value in 2021, when prices rose faster than ever
≈⅔of US households struck by COVID-19 faced financial trouble
20%down payment: buyers without it pay PMI to become homeowners
80%loan-to-value: below it, PMI can be removed, sooner if the home appreciated
Homebot’s customers are loan officers and realtors. Telling homeowners about savings helps them build trust as advisors.

02 · Define

≈$200M

a month in potential savings: ≈1M eligible Homebot homeowners × ≈$195 each.

An estimate from interviews and our data warehouse, not realised savings.

03 · Develop

Prototype widely, then test with 20 people.

I sketched and rapidly prototyped variations on copy, infographics and visualisations, and on ways to connect with loan officers and realtors. Usability sessions with 20 participants landed on a design rated 4.8/5 for value and confidence in removal.

Left: iterations on copy, visuals and contact options. Right: handoff, light mode included.

04 · Deliver

Four sprints, each valuable on its own.

With the engineering team leads, I broke the design into the smallest chunks of deliverable value. In the first sprint alone, homeowners sent 500+ requests to learn about cancelling PMI, worth around $97,500 a month.
