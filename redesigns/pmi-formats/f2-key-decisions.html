The bet

Homeowners were paying for insurance they could already drop

PMI lets buyers without a 20% down payment become homeowners. It can be removed once the loan-to-value ratio falls below 80% of the original appraised value, or sooner if the home has appreciated.

Rising prices meant many homeowners may already have crossed that line without realising it.

Key decisions

Three calls that shaped it

  1. 01

    Which opportunity is worth building?

    What I did
    Followed where interviews and our data warehouse agreed: helping homeowners realise the savings on PMI, and helping loan officers and realtors build trust as their advisors.
    Evidence
    Around one million eligible homeowners on Homebot, saving about $195 a month each: roughly $200 million a month.
  2. 02

    How do we tell someone they might be overpaying?

    What I did
    Sketched and rapidly prototyped variations on copy, infographics and visualisations, and on ways to connect homeowners with their loan officers and realtors.
    Evidence
    Usability sessions with 20 participants; the final design was rated 4.8 out of 5 on its value and on confidence in removing PMI.
  3. 03

    How do we get it to homeowners quickly?

    What I did
    With the engineering leads, broke the design into four sprints, each the smallest chunk of deliverable value.
    Evidence
    Over 500 requests to learn about cancelling PMI within the first sprint, worth around $97,500 a month in savings.

Bringing people along

One story for the whole company

  • I presented the project to the whole company using the Double Diamond framework. It connected colleagues to our mission, showed how the product org works, and built excitement for the feature.

Outcome

500 requests in the first sprint
