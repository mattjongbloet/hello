When can you remove your mortgage insurance?

Interviews and our data warehouse pointed at the same opportunity: around a million Homebot homeowners could stop paying private mortgage insurance (PMI) early. I designed the feature that told them, and connected them with their loan officers and realtors.

background

Why it mattered

Three things were true in 2021–22:

Home prices rose faster than ever in 2021. The typical home gained $50,000.
Nearly two-thirds of US households struck by COVID-19 faced financial trouble.
PMI comes off once loan-to-value drops below 80% of the original appraisal, or sooner if the home appreciated.
