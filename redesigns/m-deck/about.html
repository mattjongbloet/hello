About

Design with a purpose.

I’m Matt, a Senior Staff Product Designer in Amsterdam. I always knew I wanted to use design to make people’s lives a little bit better. Here’s the route.

Four stops, then credentials.

01 · Canterbury, England 🧙

Five years of nice-looking things without a purpose.

My career started in visual and brand design. It looked good. It didn’t feel like enough.

This is what pushed me towards research and product.

02 · Amsterdam, NL 🇳🇱

An MSc in Digital Design, Cum Laude.

Lab, field and showroom research, and new methods tried on real projects for large companies. Then I wanted somewhere I could ship dev-ready designs and run research.

Later added a UX certification (UXC) from Nielsen Norman Group.

03 · United States 🇺🇸

5+ years of pivotal roles at early- and mid-stage SaaS.

After marrying my now wife, I moved to the US: Homebot, then Staff Product Designer, Growth at Trellis.

Homebot is where PMI, Home Search, Internal Admin and Scaling Research happened.

04 · Amsterdam, NL · now

Senior Staff Product Designer at Hospitable.

Helping hosts run their short-term rentals on autopilot. Before that, Lead Product Designer at Carv.
