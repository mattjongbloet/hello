01

Executive summary

Our data showed that about a million Homebot homeowners could stop paying private mortgage insurance (PMI) early.

I designed the feature that told them, and that pointed them back to their loan officers and realtors, Homebot’s customers. It turned a homeowner’s saving into a reason for their advisor to reach out.

I took the work from prototypes through usability testing to a four-sprint delivery plan with engineering, then presented it to the whole company.

02

Market conditions

Interviews and our data warehouse pointed to the same opportunity: help homeowners realise PMI savings, and help Homebot’s customers build trust as their advisors. Three conditions made it timely:

  • Home prices rose faster than ever in 2021. The typical home gained $50,000 in value.
  • Nearly two-thirds of US households struck by COVID-19 faced financial trouble.
  • PMI lets buyers without a 20% down payment become homeowners. It can be removed once the loan-to-value ratio falls below 80% of the original appraised value, or sooner if the home has appreciated.
