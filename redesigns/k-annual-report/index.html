Letter to stakeholders

Dear hiring manager,

I’ve spent more than ten years in product design, most recently at early- and mid-stage SaaS companies, and today as Senior Staff Product Designer at Hospitable, where we help hosts run their short-term rentals on autopilot.

The work I’m proudest of happens above the screen. At Homebot our data showed a ≈$200 million a month opportunity1; I designed the feature, planned its delivery with engineering leads, and presented it to the whole company. I empowered 30+ product people to learn in rigorous ways, and built the story and prototype that helped convince investors at Human API.

I’m rigorously curious and I enjoy simplifying the complex. I’m looking for my next senior staff role, where that combination moves a whole product, not just a feature.

MattMatt Jongbloet, Senior Staff Product Designer
