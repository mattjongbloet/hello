PMI savings: helping homeowners drop mortgage insurance early

◫ Company
Homebot
◷ Date
July 2022
◎ Team
Consumer Team
✎ My part
PrototypingUsability researchDelivery planning
📌

TL;DR About 1 million Homebot homeowners were eligible to remove private mortgage insurance (PMI) early, worth ≈$195 a month each. I prototyped and tested how to tell them, with 20 participants rating the design 4.8/5. The first sprint drove 500+ requests to learn about PMI removal.

Overview

Interviews and our data warehouse pointed to the same opportunity: help homeowners realise savings on PMI, and help our customers, their loan officers and realtors, build trust as their advisors. interviews + data warehouse

We estimated that around one million US homeowners using Homebot were eligible to remove PMI early, saving each around $195 a month. Together, roughly $200 million a month. estimate from Homebot data

Background

  • Home prices rose faster than ever in 2021. The typical home gained $50,000 in value.
  • Nearly two-thirds of US households struck by COVID-19 faced financial trouble.
  • PMI lets buyers without a 20% down payment become homeowners. It can be removed once the loan-to-value ratio falls below 80% of the original appraised value, or sooner if the home has appreciated.

Action

Prototype widely

I sketched ideas and rapidly prototyped variations on copy, infographics and visualisations, and on ways to connect with loan officers and realtors.

Test with 20 people

I ran usability sessions with 20 participants and landed on a design that scored highly on its value and on confidence in removing PMI. usability testing, n = 20

4.8 / 5 for value and confidence in removal.

Slice the delivery

With the engineering team leads, I broke the design into four sprints, each the smallest chunk of deliverable value.

Outcome

MeasureResult
Requests to learn about PMI cancellation, first sprint500+
Monthly savings from those requests 500 × ≈$195≈$97,500
Usability rating, value and confidence n = 204.8 / 5

I presented the project to the whole company using the Double Diamond framework. It connected colleagues to our mission, showed how the product org works, and built excitement for the feature.
