Private mortgage insurance

Homebot, Consumer Team, July 2022. Our data showed ≈1M homeowners could stop paying private mortgage insurance early. I designed the feature that told them, and connected them with their loan officers and realtors, Homebot’s customers.

Eligible≈1Mhomeowners
Opportunity (est.)≈$200M/ month
Usability4.8/5n = 20
Sprint one500+requests
View AProduct · as designed
View B · System schematic
  1. Home prices rose faster than ever in 2021. The typical home gained $50,000 in value.
  2. Nearly two-thirds of US households struck by COVID-19 faced financial trouble.
  3. PMI lets buyers without a 20% down payment become homeowners. It can be removed once loan-to-value falls below 80% of the original appraised value, or sooner if the home has appreciated.
  4. ≈$200M a month is an estimate (≈1M eligible × ≈$195), not realised savings.
View BGeneral notes 1–4
View C · Construction sequence
  1. Stage 1Explore

    Sketched and rapidly prototyped copy, infographics, visualisations and ways to connect with loan officers and realtors.

  2. Stage 2Validate

    Usability sessions with 20 participants. Final design rated 4.8/5 on value and confidence.

  3. Stage 3Plan

    Four sprints with the engineering team leads, each the smallest chunk of deliverable value.

  4. Stage 4Align

    Presented to the whole company with the Double Diamond, connecting colleagues to the mission.

In the first sprint, homeowners sent 500+ requests to learn about cancelling PMI, worth around $97,500 a month in savings.
