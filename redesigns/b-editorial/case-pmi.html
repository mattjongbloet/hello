Finding a million homeowners a way out of mortgage insurance

Interviews and our data pointed to the same opportunity. Homeowners could save on PMI, and their loan officers and realtors could earn their trust by helping.

CompanyHomebot
TeamConsumer Team
DateJuly 2022
My partPrototyping, research, delivery plan

We estimated that around one million US homeowners using Homebot were eligible to remove private mortgage insurance early. Each could save around $195 a month. Added together, that was roughly $200 million a month that homeowners could keep.

≈$195estimated monthly saving per eligible homeowner

Homebot’s customers are the loan officers and realtors who send it to their clients. A feature that told homeowners about money they could save, and pointed them back to their advisor, could help both sides.

Background

  • Home prices rose faster than ever in 2021. The typical home gained $50,000 in value.
  • Nearly two-thirds of US households struck by COVID-19 faced financial trouble.
  • PMI lets buyers without a 20% down payment become homeowners. It can be removed once the loan-to-value ratio falls below 80% of the original appraised value, or sooner if the home has appreciated.
80%the loan-to-value line below which PMI can come off

What I did

I sketched ideas and rapidly prototyped variations on copy, infographics and visualisations, and on ways for homeowners to connect with their loan officers and realtors.

Then I ran usability sessions with 20 participants, asking how valuable the feature was and how confident they felt about removing PMI.

4.8

out of 5 for value and confidence in removal, across 20 usability sessions.

With the engineering leads, I broke the design into four sprints. Each sprint was the smallest chunk of value we could deliver on its own.

Outcome

Within the first sprint, homeowners sent more than 500 requests to learn about cancelling PMI, worth around $97,500 a month in savings.

500+requests in the first sprint

I presented the project to the whole company using the Double Diamond framework. It connected colleagues to our mission, showed how the product org works, and built excitement for the feature. ■
