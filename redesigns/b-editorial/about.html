About

Nice-looking things weren’t enough.

How five years of brand design sent me back to school for research, across the Atlantic, and home to Amsterdam.

Hello, I’m Matt . I always knew I wanted to use design to make people’s lives a little bit better. But the first five years of my career, in visual and brand design, left me feeling like I was making nice-looking things without a purpose.

So I left Canterbury, England, for an MSc in Digital Design in Amsterdam. There I learned the fundamentals of lab, field and showroom research, and had the chance to experiment with new methods on real projects for large companies. My next step was to find a place where I could deliver dev-ready designs and also conduct research.

Then I got lucky

After marrying my now wife, I moved to the USA, where I played pivotal roles in design for more than five years at early- and mid-stage SaaS companies, including Homebot and Trellis.

I’ve since moved back to Amsterdam. These days I lead design at Hospitable, helping hosts run their short-term rentals on autopilot. ■
