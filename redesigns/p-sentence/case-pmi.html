Overview

An opportunity hiding in our data

Interviews and our data warehouse pointed to the same opportunity: help homeowners realise the savings they could make on private mortgage insurance (PMI), and help our customers, their loan officers and realtors, build trust as their advisors.

We estimated that around one million US homeowners using Homebot were eligible to remove PMI early, saving each of them around $195 a month. Together that came to roughly $200 million a month.

Background

Why it mattered in 2022

  • Home prices rose faster than ever in 2021. The typical home gained $50,000 in value.
  • Nearly two-thirds of US households struck by COVID-19 faced financial trouble.
  • PMI lets buyers without a 20% down payment become homeowners. It can be removed once the loan-to-value ratio falls below 80% of the original appraised value, or sooner if the home has appreciated.

Rising prices meant many homeowners may already have crossed that line without realising it.

What I did

Prototype widely, test hard, ship in slices

  • Explored broadly. I sketched and rapidly prototyped variations on copy, infographics and visualisations, and on ways to connect homeowners with their loan officers and realtors.
  • Tested with 20 people. I ran usability sessions with 20 participants and landed on a design rated 4.8 out of 5 on its value and on confidence in removing PMI.
  • Planned delivery with engineering. With the engineering leads I broke the design into four sprints, each the smallest chunk of deliverable value.
Outcome

500 requests in the first sprint

  • Within the first sprint, homeowners sent over 500 requests to learn more about cancelling PMI, worth around $97,500 a month in savings.
  • I presented the project to the whole company using the Double Diamond framework. It connected colleagues to our mission, showed how the product org works, and built excitement for the feature.
