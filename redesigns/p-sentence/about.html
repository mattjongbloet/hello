My story

The first five years of my career, in visual and brand design, left me feeling like I was making nice-looking things without a purpose.

So I left Canterbury, England for an MSc in Digital Design in Amsterdam. There I learned the fundamentals of lab, field and showroom research, and experimented with new methods on real projects for large companies. My next step was to find a place where I could deliver dev-ready designs and also conduct research.

I got lucky. After marrying my now wife, I moved to the USA, where I played pivotal design roles for 5+ years at early- and mid-stage SaaS companies. I’ve since moved back to Amsterdam, where I help hosts run their short-term rentals on autopilot at Hospitable.
