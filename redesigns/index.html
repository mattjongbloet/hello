Notes

  • Every variation is built from A’s own files by tools/build-a-variants.py. Change A and re-run it, and all five follow.
  • Shared in A1–A5: your Hospitable role reads Senior Staff Product Designer, and the opening line leads with that role rather than UX research. A itself still shows the live site’s wording.
  • Fonts are your files, served from redesigns/fonts/: Roobert (trial) and Apercu Pro. Neither has arrows (→ ↗ ↓), so those come from Hanken Grotesk. Apercu has no italics here, so A1–A3 don’t use any.
  • The font files are not committed to the repository, because it is public and font licences don’t allow redistribution. Without them, the pages fall back to Hanken Grotesk.
  • The five PMI layouts are built by tools/build-pmi-formats.py from one set of copy. Nothing was added to your case study; the “key decisions” and “Double Diamond” layouts only regroup what’s already there.
  • Case studies are built by tools/build-cases.py. Trellis visuals are cropped from your “product investment” deck (June 2023); the $3.5MM is worded as predicted gross annual revenue, as you described it.
  • A6–A8 are built from A by tools/build-a-cv.py. Copy and numbers come from your 2024 CV; your title is Senior Staff Product Designer at Hospitable. Trellis and Homebot dates follow the CV; Hospitable shows “Now” without a start month.
  • P is built by tools/build-p.py. The status chip reads “Open to senior staff roles”; change or remove it in that file.
  • The other 14 directions (B–O) are kept in the archive.
